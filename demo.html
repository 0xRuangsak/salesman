About & Terms

What is this?
A parody game about the salesman skill, which makes an AI coding agent say more. You ask for one small thing. Salesman does it, then keeps going.
Am I talking to a real model?
No. Every reply is written by hand and the path is the same every time. The prompt you pick only changes the wording.
Is this Opusfived?
No, but it is a tribute. Opusfived is a parody of an agent that never does the thing. This is a parody of an agent that does the thing and cannot stop talking about it.
Who made it?
The salesman repo, which is itself a parody of caveman. The rules salesman follows in this game are the rules in its skill file.
Are the token counts real?
They are estimates: words times 1.3, which is the ratio the repo's own benchmark shows for salesman prose. The measured numbers come from benchmarks/bench.py.
Affiliated with anyone?
No.
Privacy?
Nothing leaves your browser. One local storage entry saves your place. "Try again" clears it.
License?
Apache-2.0, like the rest of the repo. Reuse as you wish.
